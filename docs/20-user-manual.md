# 20 — User Manual (Data Processor)

This guide is written for a **Data Processor** — the person who works the lead queue day to day. No technical background is assumed.

## 1. How to Start

1. Open the app's web address in your browser (ask your admin for the link).
2. You'll land on the **Login** page.

## 2. How to Log In

1. Enter your **username or email**.
2. Enter your **password**.
3. Click **Login**.
4. If you forgot your password, there is no "forgot password" link — ask an admin to reset it for you.

## 3. How to Navigate

After logging in, you'll see a top bar with your name and a few links:

- **Dashboard** — where you do your work (this is where you land by default).
- **My Statistics** — your own completion numbers.
- **Profile** — your account details and password change.
- **Logout** — sign out.

## 4. How to Use the Dashboard (Your Main Screen)

When you open the Dashboard, you're automatically given one business lead to work on. Nobody else can be given that same lead while you have it.

You'll see:

- **Name, Rating, Phone, Map URL, Website** for the business.
- An **Outreach Message** ready to send, with the business's name already filled in.

### Step by step for one lead

1. **Copy the phone number** — click **Copy** next to the phone field, then dial it in your phone/dialer app.
2. **Verify the listing** (optional but recommended) — click the **Verified** toggle; it opens the Google Maps listing in a new tab so you can confirm it's the right business.
3. **Check the website** (if there is one) — click **OPEN ↗** to view it in a new tab.
4. **Get your message ready** — the outreach message box shows a ready-to-send message with the business's name already inserted. Use the **◁ / ▷** arrows to see other message options (there may be several, including different languages). Click **Copy message** to copy it, then paste it into WhatsApp, SMS, or whatever channel you use.
5. **Record the outcome**, using the four buttons at the bottom:
   - **Skip** — you couldn't complete this one right now (no answer, wrong number, come back to it later). It goes back into the shared pool for anyone (including you) to pick up again later.
   - **Done and Next Name** — you finished this one. It's locked permanently as done and you move to the next lead.
   - **Next** — move on without marking anything; the lead goes back to the pool unchanged.
   - **Previous** — go back one step to the lead you were just on (useful if you clicked Next too fast or need to fix something). This works even if it means taking it back from whoever's on it now — use it to correct a recent mistake, not to browse around.
6. The screen automatically shows you the next available lead — repeat.

### When there's nothing left

If every lead has been worked, you'll see an **"All done"** message with a **Refresh Queue** button — click it to check again (useful if an admin just imported more leads).

## 5. How to Interpret Results

- A **green "Done" badge** on a lead means it's finished and locked — nobody, including you, can reopen it.
- A **"Skipped" badge** means it's back in the shared pool.
- The **"Row X / Y"** counter at the top shows your position within the current file, not the whole queue.
- **"Done today"** at the top counts how many you've personally finished today.

## 6. How to Check Your Own Performance

Go to **My Statistics** to see:
- How many you've completed today, this week, this month, and in total.
- Your average completions per hour today.
- How many you've skipped in the last 30 days.
- How many leads are still available for anyone to pick up.
- A 14-day bar chart of your daily completions.

## 7. How to Change Your Password

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Enter your current password and a new password (at least 8 characters).
3. Save.

## 8. Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Couldn't save — try again" toast appears | Just try the action again — this usually means a brief connection hiccup, or someone else's session claimed the same lead a moment before yours (rare). |
| Login says "Too many login attempts" | Wait about 15 minutes before trying again. |
| Login says "This account has been disabled" | Contact an admin. |
| The **Previous** button is greyed out | You're on the very first row of the current file — there's nothing before it to go back to. |
| I skipped a lead by mistake | It's back in the shared pool; anyone (including you, on your next claim) can pick it up again. It isn't lost. |
